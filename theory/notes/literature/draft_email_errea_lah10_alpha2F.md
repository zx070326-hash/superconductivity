# 致 Ion Errea 的数据请求（草稿，待用户决定是否发送）

收件人：Ion Errea (ion.errea@ehu.eus)
可选抄送：José A. Flores-Livas（Errea 2020 共同作者，常为 SSCHA/Eliashberg 数据联系人；地址待补）
主题：Request for LaH₁₀ Eliashberg spectral functions α²F(ω) — Errea et al., Nature 578, 66 (2020)

---

Dear Professor Errea,

I am Xin Zheng, an undergraduate student at Chu Kochen Honors College, Zhejiang University, and affiliated with the University's newly established high-pressure research center. I am studying the pressure dependence of the strong-coupling upper bound on Tc in hydrides, using the lattice-dynamical stability bound on the electron–phonon coupling λ_* introduced by Semenok, Altshuler and Yuzbashyan.

Your 2020 Nature paper on LaH₁₀ (578, 66) provides what is, to my knowledge, the only fully self-consistent SSCHA sequence of (ω_log, λ, Tc) across pressure, which makes it central to this analysis. The data-availability statement indicates the underlying data are available on request, so I am writing to ask whether it would be possible to obtain the isotropic Eliashberg spectral functions α²F(ω) computed for LaH₁₀ — ideally the anharmonic (SSCHA) ones, tabulated as (ω, α²F(ω)) — at the pressures reported in the paper, in particular near 129, 163, 214 and 264 GPa.

My reason: I currently reconstruct only the spectral second moment ω̄₂/ω_log by inverting the Allen–Dynes formula, which leaves the shape of α²F under-determined and introduces a ~10–15% spread in the derived stability bound λ_* depending on the assumed spectral shape. Direct access to the computed α²F(ω) would let me evaluate the functional λ_*[α²F] exactly rather than through a spectral-shape proxy, and pin down its pressure dependence for LaH₁₀.

Any convenient format — data files, tables, or the source data behind the figures — would be very helpful, and I would of course cite the paper. Thank you very much for considering this.

With best regards,
Xin Zheng
Chu Kochen Honors College, Zhejiang University
