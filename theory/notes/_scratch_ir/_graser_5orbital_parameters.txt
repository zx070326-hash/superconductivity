TYPICAL PARAMETER RANGES (Literature Standard):
----------------------------------------------
Orbitals: dxy, dxz, dyz, d3z²-r², dx²-y²

On-site energies ε_d:
  - Splitting typically ~1 eV between orbitals
  - d3z²-r² usually highest (~0.5 eV)
  - dxy often at Fermi level (set to 0)

Hopping integrals t_ij:
  - Nearest-neighbor: 0.1-0.5 eV
  - Next-nearest-neighbor: 0.05-0.2 eV
  - Strong anisotropy between in-plane and out-of-plane

Interactions:
  - Hubbard U: 1.0-2.0 eV (typical ~1.5 eV)
  - Hund's J: 0.2-0.3 eV (typical ~0.25 eV)
  - Chemical potential μ: adjusted to match filling

BAND STRUCTURE FEATURES:
-----------------------
- Hole pockets at Γ point (center of Brillouin zone)
- Electron pockets at M points (corners)
- Weak kz dispersion (quasi-2D)
