PASSes at every one of the 32 (N,param) cases in the full sweep at this pad (0 FAILs), and it resolves

cases (s in {0.99,1.0,1.01} at both N=6,8) have TRUE float64 gaps at the 1e-13-1e-15 level or exactly

finite-size-symmetry-protected) degeneracy at this specific value s=1, not a precision artifact. No pad
choice fixes this; it would need either a symmetry-sector decomposition (splitting the exact degeneracy
analytically) or accepting "inconclusive at s=1" as the honest, correct answer.
